About the Founder

Vivian Park (MJ)

Vivian is an English educator and language education researcher based in Cambridge, UK. She has over 15 years of experience supporting children's English development through reading, writing and meaningful language experiences.

She completed an MPhil in Education (Research in Second Language Education) at the University of Cambridge and continues her research in multilingual children's language learning, home environments and digitally mediated literacy practices.

From a parent's observation to research-informed practice

Vivian English began from a simple question: how can children experience English naturally and confidently at home? Through teaching practice and research, Vivian connects family language environments, children's interests and structured literacy development.
